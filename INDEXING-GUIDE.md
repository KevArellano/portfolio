# Get Your Site Indexed - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 Goal
Get kevarellano.com ranking #1 for "kevarellano" and "Kevin Arellano" searches.

## Step 1: Google Search Console (CRITICAL - Do This First)

### Setup (5 minutes)
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Start now" and sign in with your Google account
3. Click "Add property" → Enter: `kevarellano.com`
4. Choose verification method:

#### Option A: HTML File Upload (Easiest)
- Download the verification file (e.g., `google1234567890abcdef.html`)
- Upload it to your portfolio folder
- Deploy to Netlify
- Click "Verify" in Search Console

#### Option B: DNS Verification (Recommended if you manage DNS)
- Add the TXT record to your domain's DNS settings
- Wait 5-10 minutes for DNS propagation
- Click "Verify"

### After Verification
1. Submit your sitemap:
   - In Search Console, go to "Sitemaps" (left sidebar)
   - Enter: `sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"

2. Request indexing:
   - Go to "URL Inspection" (top bar)
   - Enter: `https://kevarellano.com/`
   - Click "Request Indexing"
   - Wait 1-7 days for Google to crawl

## Troubleshooting

### Site Not Indexed After 7 Days?
1. Check Search Console for crawl errors
2. Verify robots.txt allows crawling: https://kevarellano.com/robots.txt
3. Check sitemap is accessible: https://kevarellano.com/sitemap.xml
4. Request indexing again

### Ranking Lower Than Expected?
1. Add more content (blog posts)
2. Get more backlinks (GitHub, LinkedIn, dev.to)
3. Ensure your name appears naturally in content
4. Wait - SEO takes time (2-8 weeks for new sites)
